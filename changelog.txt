Version: 2.1.3
Date: 2026-09-07
  Bugfixes:
    - Stop crashing on Vulcanus. A demolisher is a chain of segment entities, and
      destroying any one of them destroys the whole creature -- so clearing a chunk that
      held one left the mod holding invalid handles to the rest, and it died.
    - What you have built is no longer destroyed when a chunk is mirrored. A chunk on the
      far side of a mirror line is blanked while it waits for its partner to be generated,
      and anything standing there when the copy finally arrives was being wiped along with
      the blank ground. The PVP scenario walks straight into this: it generates each team's
      chunks and then builds their base, so a base could be built during that wait and die
      when the copy landed. Reported as issue #1. The terrain is still mirrored either way,
      so both halves of the map continue to match.

Version: 2.1.2
Date: 2026-09-07
  Bugfixes:
    - The chunk offset can no longer be set below zero. Its floor was written as
      min_value, which is not a field, so nothing enforced it.
    - The X and Y axis settings describe the right halves of the map. Their descriptions
      were swapped: mirroring on X makes east and west match, not north and south.
    - Decoratives are mirrored rather than grown afresh. They were regenerated from the
      reflection's own noise, which was the standing TODO -- and since the regeneration
      ran before the chunk was marked generated, it did nothing at all, so a mirrored
      chunk came out bare. Now every decorative is copied to its reflected position, in
      the same numbers.
    - Changing an axis or the chunk offset during a game now takes effect. The settings
      were read once when the mod loaded, so a change did nothing until the save was
      reloaded -- and a client who joined after one was working from different numbers
      than everyone already playing.

Version: 2.1.0
Date: 2026-09-07
  Other:
    - Update for Factorio 2.1
  Bugfixes:
    - Stop crashing the moment a chunk is mirrored: 2.0 moved the prototype tables off
      game, so game.decorative_prototypes no longer exists
    - Only mirror worlds the map generator made. Space age brought surfaces that are not
      worlds, and this mod acted on every one of them: a space platform is a surface like
      any other, and blanking a chunk of one would have deleted the platform out from
      under whoever was standing on it. Other planets are still mirrored -- they are
      worlds, and mirroring the world is the point.
